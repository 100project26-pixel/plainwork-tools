Sort defects without hiding safety risk.

Safety rule built into this tool

If a defect may affect safe operation, braking, steering, structural integrity, guarding, fire risk, required safety equipment or legal compliance, do not use a numeric score to justify continued operation. Remove or restrict the equipment from service as applicable and have a qualified person assess it under manufacturer instructions, workplace procedures and governing requirements.
