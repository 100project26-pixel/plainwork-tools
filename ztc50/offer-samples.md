# Zero-to-Cash 50 — minimum sellable samples

These are deliberately small examples used to test whether a buyer wants a paid version.

## Z-001 Spreadsheet Cleanup Sprint
Before: mixed date formats, duplicate rows, inconsistent categories, hidden formulas, no filter.  
After deliverable: standardized table, duplicates flagged/removed, obvious formula issues repaired, filters/validation added, one-page change summary.

## Z-002 PDF → Spreadsheet
Input example: a recurring 3-page supplier statement.  
Deliverable schema: `date | document_no | description | qty | unit_price | amount | source_page | review_note`.

## Z-003 Competitor Price Snapshot
Deliverable schema: `competitor | offer | listed_price | unit_basis | included | minimum | delivery/terms | source_url | checked_at | note`.  
Executive summary: 5 pricing findings, 3 gaps, 3 actions.

## Z-004 Website Copy Refresh
Deliverable: current-page diagnosis, rewritten hero/value proposition, service section, proof/FAQ prompts and CTA. Claims are not invented.

## Z-009 Lead Research Pack
Deliverable schema: `company | website | location | fit_reason | public_contact_page | public_business_email_if_listed | evidence | notes`. No personal/private contact scraping.

## Z-012 SOP From Notes
Deliverable: purpose, scope, owner, prerequisites, numbered procedure, checkpoints, exceptions, records and revision date.

## Z-019 Competitor Review Mining
Deliverable: review sample, recurring praise, recurring complaints, frequency estimate, representative paraphrases, opportunity themes and recommended tests.

## Z-024 KPI Dashboard Setup
Deliverable: cleaned source tab, metric definitions, formulas, simple summary tab and update instructions.

## Z-028 Resume Rewrite
Deliverable: fact-preserving role-targeted resume, keyword alignment notes and change summary. No fabricated credentials or experience.

## Z-046 Newsletter Research Pack
Deliverable: 8–12 sourced facts/examples, 5 angles, 10 headline options and a clean source list.
